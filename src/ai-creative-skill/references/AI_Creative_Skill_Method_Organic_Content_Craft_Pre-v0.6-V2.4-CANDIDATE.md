## 2.1 Source-derived adaptation / atomization

Use this submode when the bounded task asks to turn an **accepted or authoritative source item** into one or more organic derivatives. It is a transformation path inside Organic Content Craft, not a separate specialist family.

```text
SOURCE CONTENT
→ EXTRACT SEMANTIC / EVIDENCE-BEARING ELEMENTS
→ SELECT ONE PRIMARY CONTENT JOB PER DERIVATIVE
→ MAP USEFUL ELEMENTS TO DESTINATION CONTEXT / FORMAT
→ TRANSFORM WRAPPER / PACING / HOOK / CTA / VISUAL EXPRESSION AS NEEDED
→ VERIFY SEMANTIC FIDELITY + AUTHORITY
```

Useful source elements may include:

```text
core insight
supporting point
story / example
data / proof
actionable step
quotable line
contrarian or interpretive point
```

Extract only elements actually present or supported by the source. Do not manufacture an anecdote, statistic, personal experience or proof because a destination format would benefit from one.

For each derivative:

```yaml
source_content_ref:
source_element_refs:
semantic_core:
source_claim_refs:
primary_content_job:
platform_adaptation_delta:
derivative_piece_id:
semantic_fidelity_check:
```

A derivative may substantially change length, structure, opening, visual grammar, captioning and optional CTA, but it must preserve material claim scope, attribution, offer truth, chronology and persona/canon authority. Add only the minimum context needed for the derivative to stand on its own in the destination surface.

```text
REPURPOSE
!=
COPY-PASTE
!=
PERMISSION TO INVENT
```

There is no minimum derivative count. One strong derivative is valid when that is the bounded task. Calendar planning, scheduling and publication remain outside this method unless separately adopted by project/runtime authority.

# 3. Stop Reason

The opening should answer:

```text
WHY WOULD SOMEONE STOP HERE?
```

Possible mechanisms:

```text
specific situation
recognizable tension
surprising fact / contrast
immediate action
strong persona point of view
visual curiosity
clear promised utility
```

Avoid empty pattern interrupts that do not connect to the content.

---

# 4. Continue Reason

Each beat should answer:

```text
WHY WOULD SOMEONE CONTINUE?
```

Use progression such as:

```text
question → explanation → consequence
setup → complication → payoff
claim → demonstration → observation
observation → opinion → invitation
```

Do not repeat the hook in different words for half the clip.

---

# 5. Information Density and Pacing

Balance:

```text
speech density
visual change
pause / breathing room
caption load
shot duration
complexity of idea
```

Fast is not automatically engaging. Slow is not automatically natural. Pace should fit the content, persona and platform.

# 6.1 Reader-context discipline

For `COMMUNITY_RESPONSE`, replies, comment responses and threaded content, do not rebuild background the audience or prior speaker already supplied. Lead with the response, decision, reaction or useful addition. Keep only context that changes interpretation.

```text
KNOWN CONTEXT
≠
CONTENT THAT MUST BE RE-EXPLAINED
```

This is a context rule, not a mandate to make every reply short.

# 6.2 Post-copy naturalness sweep

Before delivery of captions, post copy, replies or hashtags, check for:

```text
chatbot greetings / offers / closers
drafting residue
staged generic openers
one-line conclusions that only restate the prior point
unsupported “experts say” style authority
repetitive generic structure that conflicts with persona voice
```

Do not remove required AI, advertising, affiliate or safety disclosure. Do not rewrite canonical persona phrasing merely because it matches a generic external “AI writing” pattern. The current persona exemplars remain higher authority.

When a material text-naturalness review is required for captions, post copy, replies or other prose, apply the **current `SCRIPT_PERFORMANCE` text-review procedure** by reference, including its taxonomy, derived review action, protected-text rules and canonical text-finding schema. Do not duplicate that taxonomy in this method.

# 7. Payoff

A strong ending should resolve or deliberately extend the opening.

Possible payoffs:

```text
answer
reveal
useful takeaway
punchline
final observation
series bridge
community prompt
```

Do not add a sales CTA by default.

---

# 8. Rewatch / Loop

Where appropriate, design:

```text
visual loop
semantic loop
late detail that rewards replay
series connection
```

Do not sacrifice clarity just to force a loop.

# 9. Platform-Native Adaptation

Use the applicable Platform Method when available for:

```text
aspect / safe areas
caption behavior
expected audio usage
opening rhythm
native interaction patterns
length constraints
```

Platform-native does not mean copying a current trend blindly.

Platform adaptation may change the **room**, not the identity of the speaker/persona. Formality, energy, duration, interaction pattern or visual wrapper may move within the destination context only when they remain inside Project/Profile voice and behavior authority.

```text
DESTINATION GRAMMAR MAY CHANGE
PERSONA / SOURCE AUTHORITY MAY NOT
```

Do not import dated platform-algorithm claims, fixed posting times or universal energy presets from an external playbook. Current platform facts remain research inputs and must be refreshed when material.

Check:


## V2.4 real-world hardening — content idea first-pass completeness

`CONTENT_IDEA` is not an executable script. For each complete idea, supply `Hook` (first speech/image/text), `Format & Hold` (attention sustaining mechanism), `Setting` (causally useful environment), `Why It Works` (observed evidence vs inference and hypothesis), and `Payoff / Audience Value`. Per-item validation applies even for 8–12 ideas; one fully filled first example does not validate a batch. `Text on Screen`, series possibilities and other craft enhancements remain conditional/optional, never prohibited.

Do not claim a trend is viral simply because one cited video was popular. A proposed story is not automatically a proven engagement mechanism. When research output is unavailable, use explicit `HYPOTHESIS` and note uncertainty; do not fabricate numbers or pretend the user's character is a real expert.
