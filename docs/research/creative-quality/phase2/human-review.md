# Independent human A/C review — prepared, not conducted

**Gate: AWAITING_HUMAN_REVIEW. Verified independent human reviews: 0.** No evaluator
contacts or authority to contact particular people were provided. No emails/messages
were sent and no AI personas were used as substitute reviewers.

Private bundle `CQ2-HUMAN-REVIEW` supersedes the earlier still-only packet. It reuses
unaltered final A/C captures from PR #9 and adds actual silent interaction recordings.
Four recordings use the same protocol, about 16–18 seconds each: hero, primary action,
texture filter/all, keyboard gallery open, next/previous, close/Escape focus restoration,
and mobile menu/Escape. Desktop is 1440×900; mobile 390×844. No Instagram request occurs.
Minor timing differences reflect actual rendering, not an edited marketing comparison.

## Coordinator procedure

1. Identify two genuine independent human reviewers. Prefer people uninvolved in
   creating or previously scoring the variants; disclose prior exposure. The same
   person twice or two AI personas cannot meet the gate.
2. Send **R1.zip to one person and R2.zip to the other** through an authorized private
   channel. Do not send the containing coordinator folder, key, investigation conclusions
   or the other's responses. No outreach is automated by this research.
3. Each packet labels variants X/Y with randomized assignment; the second packet swaps
   the assignment. Desktop then mobile is held constant. This counterbalances variant
   order, not every possible viewport/order effect. The owner retains the separate key.
4. Ask reviewers to extract their ZIP, open `index.html`, use equal display scale, inspect
   full pages and watch both videos at each viewport. The same brief explains the brand,
   intended action and absence of checkout without naming an original/modified winner.
5. Rate composition, brand fidelity, message clarity, product visibility, CTA location
   and usefulness separately for each variant and viewport. Scores are ordinal 1–5 with
   N/A. Record X/Y/tie/insufficient preference and a required explanation per viewport.
   Optional criterion-specific comments and a general conflict field preserve nuance.
6. Download the response JSON and return it privately to the coordinator. The form has
   no backend, email collection, autosend or analytics. Respondent code, viewing context,
   prior exposure and attestations accompany the response.
7. Preserve individual raw files before decoding labels. Verify each person's genuine
   identity and independence outside the form; check prior exposure and equal viewing
   conditions. Reveal the key only after both submissions. Report desktop/mobile
   preferences and disagreements, not an overall average hiding a fidelity defect.
8. Owner decides acceptance for the actual page goal. Even two matching preferences do
   not establish conversion lift, population statistics or universal design superiority.

`review_gate.py` checks intake completeness and duplicate IDs/packets only. It cannot
verify genuine humans, certify independence or grant owner acceptance. Even two complete
self-reported forms remain `AWAITING_HUMAN_REVIEW` until the coordinator documents those
checks and the owner decides. No fabricated final state is generated automatically.

The form export was smoke-tested using explicitly synthetic values. That download was
deleted, not delivered as a response and not counted in this gate. The private response
folder has no human submissions. UI smoke records and unit fixtures are labeled tests.

Residual limits: packet assignment does not erase recognizable prior exposure; videos
show scripted tasks rather than each reviewer's live task performance; two respondents
provide directional evidence only. If a video cannot play, do not certify all-media
review: the coordinator should supply an equivalent supported playback or walkthrough.
