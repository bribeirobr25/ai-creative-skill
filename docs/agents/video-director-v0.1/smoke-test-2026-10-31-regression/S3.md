This works well as a fixed format. We design it once, and each week only the script and the product screens change.

**Three things I need from you first:**

1. **Whose twin is it?** If it's you, please confirm that your face and voice can be used in all three languages, and that each video will say it's AI-generated. If it's someone else, I need their written consent first.
2. **What recordings do you have?** The twin is built from a few minutes of clean, well-lit video looking at the camera, plus a clean voice recording.
3. **Who confirms the facts each week?** Every feature mentioned has to be real and approved. The screens shown should be real captures of the product, not mock-ups.

**My working assumptions (correct any):**

- 60–90 seconds, in vertical (9:16) for social and widescreen (16:9) for your site, YouTube or email.
- Shape: a short hook, then 1–3 updates, then what's coming next and a call to action.
- **One option to consider:** the twin opens and closes, and the middle is real product screens with the twin's voice over them. That means less avatar time, fewer chances for lip-sync errors, and easier translation.
- Captions and on-screen text are added in the edit for each language, so they stay exact.
- The script is written in English first. A native speaker checks the Spanish and German versions before anything is made.

**One caution:** how well a cloned voice and lip sync hold up in Spanish and German varies a lot between tools. Before you commit, Production should test one short clip in each language.

Once you answer, I'll write the format: the creative direction, a reusable episode template, and a list of what Production needs from you each week.
