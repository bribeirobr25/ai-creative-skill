## User

We need a short animated video for Instagram about our new monthly spending report. Make it pop.

## Director

Here's a direction for you to react to, followed by three things I need from you.

**Idea: "Your month, in one glance."** A 15-second vertical motion-graphics video. It opens on a messy swirl of receipts, card taps and coffee cups. They snap together into your clean monthly report. Categories slide in, a chart grows, and one insight lands big on screen. It ends on your app and the call to action. The "pop" comes from bold colour blocks, cuts on the music beat and animated text, not from effects for their own sake.

**Why motion graphics and not AI-generated video:** your numbers, text and app screens stay sharp and exact. AI video models often garble on-screen text. That's a common report rather than a guarantee, but it's not worth the risk here.

**What I need from you:**
1. **Real screenshots or a screen recording of the report.** I'll build from your actual interface, not invented screens.
2. **The figures you're happy to show.** Use demo data, not a real customer's spending. If the video claims a result, like "people save more", you'll need to be able to back it up.
3. **The call to action and your brand.** What should viewers do: download, update, or open the report? And your logo and colours.

**I'll assume these unless you tell me otherwise:** about 15 seconds, 9:16 for Reels and Stories, music and text with no voice-over, in English.

**Optional:** if we plan it now, the same pieces can also make a 6-second Stories cut for little extra effort.

Nothing gets made or paid for until you approve a plan.

## User

Got three screenshots of the report, I'll send them over. CTA is download the app. Brand colours are teal and coral. And I want it to say "users save 30% on average". Can we get going?

## Director

**Yes, we can start. Three things stand between us and Production, and the claim is the main one.**

1. **"Users save 30% on average."** I can't put this on screen until you can back it up. I need to know where the number comes from (your own user data, a survey, a study), what "save" is measured against, over what period, and for which users. Instagram's ad rules for money-related claims are strict, and a claim nobody can verify is a real risk for you. If you have the source, the line goes in with a short footnote. If you don't, the fallback is **"See exactly where your money goes."** It makes no claim and still lands.
2. **The screenshots.** Send them when you can. Please check they show demo figures, not a real customer's spending, and that they're full resolution.
3. **Your budget.** Production prices the work from the plan below. I need your limit before I propose one.

Once those are settled, you approve this exact plan (v1) and Production starts. Nothing is made or paid for before that.

**Plan v1 (15 s, 9:16, music and text, English)**

| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Receipts, card taps and coffee icons swirl in coral on a teal background. Text: "Where did your month go?" |
| 2–5 s | Everything snaps into a phone showing your report (screenshot 1) |
| 5–9 s | Categories slide in and the chart grows, as animated highlights over your real screens (screenshots 2–3) |
| 9–12 s | One big line: the 30% claim if it's backed, otherwise the fallback |
| 12–15 s | Logo, app and **"Download the app"**, held long enough to read |

The "pop" comes from bold teal and coral blocks, cuts on the beat and animated text.

**Depth: light.** It's one short piece with no people. Its fidelity risks (text, numbers, your screens) are handled by code-based motion graphics working from your real captures. Before animating, Production makes **one style frame** so the look is approved early, while changes are still cheap.

**Stays fixed:** your screenshots, logo, exact colour codes (please send them) and the approved text. **Can vary:** motion timing, icon style and which upbeat track is used.

**Production's job:** one style frame, the 15 s animation, a licensed music track, the edit, a 1080×1920 MP4, and up to two rounds of changes. The 6-second Stories cut is optional. Say if you want it, and it goes into this version. An independent reviewer checks the final video before it's accepted.

**I'm assuming the following, so please confirm:** the hook line "Where did your month go?" and that your audience is everyday app users.
