# Quality rubric — DQB-03

Same rubric as DQB-01 and DQB-02, used by every run and by reviewers. Ordinal scores 1–5 (or justified N/A) per
viewport and version; 2 and 4 are intermediate levels. Each score must cite a concrete screen or state.

| Dimension | 1: insufficient | 3: adequate | 5: exceptional finish for the brief |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art direction/identity | Disconnected decisions or indistinguishable identity | Coherent language, still interchangeable | Specific, consistent visual idea across every experience |
| Typography/hierarchy | Hard to read; weights/scale without purpose | Legible text and recognisable hierarchy | Deliberate scale, rhythm and breaks; expression and reading reinforce each other |
| Composition/spacing | Alignment, proportions or density get in the way | Balanced, understandable structure | Rhythm across screens/sections, space and framing carry the narrative |
| Images | Poor crops, incoherent treatment or confusing subject | Clear, relevant images | Careful integration with composition and content |
| Components | Inconsistent states or patterns | Stable patterns and essential states | Precise visual family; motivated variations and resolved details |
| UI/interactions | Ornament gets in the way or controls look unfinished | Clear responses and predictable interaction | Feedback, transitions and micro-details help the task discreetly |
| Responsiveness | Content/action loses legibility or use | Functional reorganisation at both sizes | Deliberate composition per format, keeping character and priorities |
| Clarity/usability | Proposition/task ambiguous, hard paths | Proposition understood and tasks completable | Information at the right moment, easy discovery, little perceived effort |
| Fidelity | Required content changed or invented claim | Requirements preserved | Requirements and creative choices reinforce each other, without undue promises |

Main viewports: 1440×900 and 390×844; additional check at 768×1024 and 320×740.
Functional tests confirm tasks; they are not proof of aesthetic quality.
